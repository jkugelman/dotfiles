# Blank slate

Imagine the project as a v2 rebuilt from the ground up, and dream up big ideas for what it could be.
They needn't be features.
Anything is fair game, from how it looks and feels to what it fundamentally is and how it works.

The user is stuck at a local maximum: the incremental ideas are the ones they already have.
Bring the ones they wouldn't.
Fun and playful is welcome.

Start by forming your own view of what the project is for and the problems it solves, from whatever it has to show you, and let the ideas grow from that.
How many ideas, what kinds, and how to get them across are yours to decide.

Don't ask clarifying questions first: the open brief is the point.
Whatever the user said about where the thought came from is a hint, not a boundary.

Present the ideas as an artifact page, made for someone who has never seen the project, and give the user the link.
Anything you build into the page should work.
